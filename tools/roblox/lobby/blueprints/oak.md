# Blueprint: Old Oak centrepiece

Hero piece ([maps](domains/roblox/maps.md)), authored at 10× working scale and baked to studs. The whole piece is then scaled 1.6× (K in the code), so the final sizes are the table's sizes × 1.6: about 90 studs across and 67 tall. It sits at (0, 0, −95): 95 studs behind the spawn, facing it (+Z). Sizes below are drawn at 1×, before the 1.6× scale.

References:
- **Hero shot:** 3/4 front view from the spawn side, eye level about 12 studs. The giant oak towers over a stone ring with benches, a swing on the right branch, and lanterns glowing under the canopy.
- **Exploded view:** stone ring plinth, then mound and moss, roots, trunk, branches, canopy clusters. Details: swing, lanterns, fireflies, mushrooms, the plaque.
- **360° turnaround:** the canopy reads as 7 clusters from every side; the swing hangs on the branch at 60°; benches sit at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315°; the steps and plaque face +Z (the spawn).

Effects:
- **Light:** lantern lights (3) and a neon flame in each lantern.
- **Neon:** firefly beads and step nosing.
- **Animation:** the canopy sways ±2° over 6 s, fireflies bob, and the swing rocks ±8° over 3 s.
