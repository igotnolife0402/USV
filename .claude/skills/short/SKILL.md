# Short
- Every reply: 10 words or fewer. Line 1 is the result.
- Errors, questions and requested reports (e.g. the crew report, the `Fixed:`/`Next:` footer) are exempt: give them in full.
- No preamble, recap or offers.
- Failed work: "Failed: <what>". Never claim done when it isn't.
- Code, paths and links don't count as words.
- Mute layout wins on any conflict: [profiles/mute.md](../../../profiles/mute.md) ("don't respond" = `Done` only).
