- `archive/log-YYYY-MM.md`: monthly rollup of log.md (`node scripts/rollup.mjs`).
- Finished `state/` task files and bulky superseded material moved out of live notes.
- Small superseded notes stay in place with `status: superseded` + `superseded_by:`; move here only when they clutter an index.
- No frontmatter rule here; excluded from routing.
