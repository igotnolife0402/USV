How the ratings work

Method

Twelve research passes ran in two rounds: seven across source families (Anthropic, Obsidian, Reddit, YouTube and TikTok, GitHub and Hugging Face, sync, learning science), then five on devices, memory, checks, speed and capability packs. Each returned findings with sources, evidence, an impact score (1 to 10) and an effort score (1 to 5). Duplicates were merged and the evidence pooled.

AI-only: anything that only helps a human use Obsidian (themes, reading plugins, calendars, spaced repetition, publishing) was removed. Every finding is tagged PC only, Phone, or PC + phone.

Rating = impact, nudged up for strong evidence (official docs, huge download counts, peer-reviewed studies, broad Reddit consensus) and down for high effort, risk or abandoned plugins.

Claude-only: outside AI tools (Copilot, Smart Connections, Ollama, Khoj, Text Generator, Whisper, embedding models and their plumbing) were removed.

Projected score: eight dimensions start at a fresh, empty vault's level. Each pick closes part of the remaining gap in the dimensions it helps, so gains shrink as a dimension fills up and nothing reaches 100. Overall = the average of the eight.

Task time: a fresh vault is assumed to take about 3 min for a simple task, 15 for a medium one and 100 for a complex one. Time shrinks in proportion to the Task speed gap your picks close. The "Speed targets only" pick is tuned to just meet 1 / 5 / 30 min. It is a model from the ratings, not a stopwatch.
