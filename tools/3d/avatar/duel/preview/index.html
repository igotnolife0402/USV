Supernova Duel Before / After

The same fight, same rigs, same effects. The bottom clip has the new cinematic floor: a slow push into the final blow, a dark dip and flash that hide the cut, and impact frames on every clash: a freeze, a half-speed recoil and a small flash.

Beats jump both clips to the same story moment
Push-in

From 12.2 s the camera creeps, then speeds into the blow (eased, not linear), with the lens tightening.

Slow-mo

Time drops to 0.6x, then 0.3x into the blow, so the last 0.9 s of the fight takes 2.5 s.

Dip + flash

The frame darkens for 4 frames, then flashes white. The wide shot cuts in under the flash.

Impact frames

Every clash freezes 0.12 s, recoils at half speed, and pops a small white flash sized to the hit (3.6, 5.4, 5.85, 6.3, 7.0 s).

Camera eases

Every shot now enters already moving and settles, instead of starting from a dead stop at each cut.

Rendered headless (software WebGL, 30 fps, 1100x616). The after clip is 3.4 s longer: slow-mo and freezes add time, the story is unchanged.
